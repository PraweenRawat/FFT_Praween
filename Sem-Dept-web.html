Welcome to the Computer Science Department

We help students build a strong foundation in programming,systems and problem solving, and get them ready for thetechnology industry through practical learning, labs and projects.

Department Introduction

The Department of Computer Science provides students with a mix of theoretical and practical knowledge. Students learn important subjects such as programming, databases, web development and other computer science technologies.

Vision

To provide quality education and develop skilled computer science professionals.

Mission

To provide students with knowledge and practical skills in computer science and technology.

Objectives

  • Improve programming skills.
  • Develop problem solving skills.
  • Provide practical knowledge.
